    PsyX is reachable from this private LAN without an account or code. Protect the network, the database and the backups.

    Cloud brain

    In cloud mode, PsyX sends the selected context for replies, reviews and portraits: conversations, profile and memory, plus Nestor notes, tasks and email log excerpts for portraits.
