# Animal sound credits

The bundled pack contains 57 clips. Each row retains its source, author,
licence and edits. Individual audio licences apply independently of the
repository's MIT code licence. Edited ShareAlike recordings retain the stated
licence. Clips are mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, with edge fades and level adjustment,
and last at most eight seconds. The mouse and yak are human imitations; the
T-Rex is an imagined effect. Labels distinguish them from animal recordings.

Additional instance-owned clips can be mounted from outside Git; see
[private sound packs](../../../../../docs/INSTALLATION.md#private-sound-packs).
Their presence never grants redistribution rights. No claim is made to original
U.S. Government works.
