Generalist Scoring Methodology

How Generalist Scores are Calculated

1. Weighted Category Sum (60% weight)

Each category contributes based on its importance for general-purpose use:

  • Core Capabilities (60%): coding (15%), reasoning (15%), factual (10%), creative (10%), instruction-following (10%)
  • Specialized (30%): math (8%), summarization (7%), multi-turn-reasoning (7%), context-retention (5%), translation (3%)
  • Quality Assurance (10%): edge-cases (5%), general (5%)

2. Coverage Penalty

Models are penalized for missing categories:

  • Up to -20 points total (scaled by missing category weights)
  • Encourages breadth across all capabilities
  • 100% coverage = no penalty

3. Consistency Bonus

Models with consistent performance across categories receive a bonus:

  • +5 points if standard deviation < 10
  • Rewards models that perform well everywhere
  • Penalizes "one-trick ponies"
Final Formula:
Generalist Score = Weighted Sum - Coverage Penalty + Consistency Bonus
