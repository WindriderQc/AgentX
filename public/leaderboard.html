Quality Board Methodology

Generalist Score Calculation

The Quality Board ranks models by their ability to perform well across ALL task categories, not just speed.

Formula:
Generalist Score = Weighted Sum - Coverage Penalty + Consistency Bonus
Category Weights (16 categories, total 100%)
  • Core Capabilities (49%): Reasoning (13%), Coding (12%), Factual (8%), Creative (8%), Instruction-Following (8%), Math (8%)
  • Specialized (19%): Summarization (7%), Multi-turn (7%), Context-Retention (5%)
  • Deep Evaluation (11%): Refactoring (3%), Debugging (3%), Explanation (3%), Dialogue (2%)
  • Quality Assurance (13%): Edge-Cases (5%), General (5%), Translation (3%)
Penalties & Bonuses
  • Coverage Penalty: -20 points max for missing categories (proportional to weight)
  • Consistency Bonus: +5 points if within-category std deviation < 15
Elite Score (Ceiling Differentiation)

Models scoring 95+ on the generalist scale are at the "ceiling" and hard to distinguish. The Elite score uses only L4+ (expert) prompts with reasoning-heavy category weights:

  • Reasoning (20%): Complex analysis and logic
  • Coding (18%): Code correctness under hard prompts
  • Math (15%): Multi-step math problems
  • Multi-turn (12%): Multi-step reasoning chains
  • Debugging (10%), Edge Cases (10%), Refactoring (8%), Context (7%)

The elite score provides meaningful spread among top-tier models that would otherwise appear tied.
