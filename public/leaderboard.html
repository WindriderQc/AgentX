Quality Board Methodology

Generalist Score Calculation

The Quality Board ranks models by their ability to perform well across ALL task categories, not just speed.

Formula:
Generalist Score = Weighted Sum - Coverage Penalty + Consistency Bonus
Category Weights (Total: 100%)
  • Core Capabilities (60%): Coding (15%), Reasoning (15%), Factual (10%), Creative (10%), Instruction-Following (10%)
  • Specialized (30%): Math (8%), Summarization (7%), Multi-turn (7%), Context-Retention (5%), Translation (3%)
  • Quality Assurance (10%): Edge-Cases (5%), General (5%)
Penalties & Bonuses
  • Coverage Penalty: -20 points max for missing categories (proportional to weight)
  • Consistency Bonus: +5 points if std deviation < 15 across categories
